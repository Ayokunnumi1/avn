# Verifier onboarding guide (Agent app, port 3001)

1. **Register** with your legal name, phone, email, date of birth (18+) and home address (used to avoid assigning your own home).
2. **Profile** → take a profile photo with the in-app camera, submit your government ID (NIN, BVN, voter card, driver's licence or passport) with a photo of the document and a selfie, and add your Nigerian bank account for payouts.
3. Wait for **approval** by the AVN team. Only approved verifiers receive paid jobs.
4. **Go available** on Home. Keep the app open while on duty — browsers cannot track location in the background; you become unavailable automatically if updates stop.
5. **Jobs** shows nearby offers (area, walking time, reward, deadline). Accept to see the exact address and directions. Only one verifier can win a job.
6. At the property: tap **I have arrived** (GPS recorded; poor GPS or an inaccessible property can be declared — it goes to review, never auto-rejected). Capture the required photos with the in-app camera (gallery uploads are not accepted). Photos queue on your phone and upload automatically when signal returns.
7. Optionally send the applicant a confirmation code. Complete the checklist honestly — if nobody is home, say so; you are paid the same and your score is not affected. Never enter private property.
8. **Submit**. Earnings appear once the visit is processed; withdraw from **Earnings** (finance approval, then bank transfer).
9. Use **Request assistance** or **Report unsafe conditions** at any time.
